How It Works

1

Configure scan region

Take one screenshot with a signature visible. In Settings, draw a box over the signature area on your HUD. Saved permanently — only needed once.

2

Set screenshot folder & start monitoring

Point the app at your Star Citizen screenshot folder and click Start Monitoring. The watcher runs silently in the background.

3

Press PrintScreen in-game

When you see a signature on your targeting HUD, take a screenshot. The scanner detects the new file automatically.

4

Read the overlay

An always-on-top popup appears over the game with the identification result — mineral name, tier, and mining method. Auto-hides after a configurable duration.

Known Limitations

  • Signature 4000 collides: Large ground deposits share 4000 with Common-tier ship-mining rocks. Use context (planet vs. space) to distinguish.
  • Signature 3000 collides: FPS small ground deposits and 890 Jump capital wreck debris share 3000. Context required.
  • Vlk Pearls, Vlk Irradiated Pearls, and Flowstone have signature 0 — undetectable by this scanner.
  • Star Citizen must be in Windowed or Borderless Windowed mode for the overlay to appear over the game.
  • First startup downloads ~115 MB of OCR model files. Subsequent startups use the local cache.
